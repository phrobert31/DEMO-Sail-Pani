Frequently asked questions

How far do you go?

Anywhere, including outside Italy. Longer routes are arranged a few days ahead.

Do you sail alone?

Usually, yes: deliveries are single-handed. When the route or the boat calls for it, we bring extra crew.

Do you take any boat?

Any type up to 20 metres, as long as it's in good condition. We don't take on boats that aren't seaworthy.

I have just bought a boat, can you collect it?

Yes. We pick it up from the yard or the broker and bring it to your port.

Do you work with marinas and agencies?

Yes, we also skipper for marinas, boatyards and charter agencies.
