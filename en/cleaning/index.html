Boat cleaning and care

Sailing and motor boats up to 30 metres, afloat or ashore. You decide whether to clean the interior only, the exterior only, or everything.

What we do

Every job is tailored: tell us what you need and we'll prepare a quote.

Exterior

  • Deck and cockpit
  • Gel coat and fibreglass
  • Portholes and windows
  • Waxing
  • Acid wash

Interior

  • Cabins, saloon, galley
  • Heads and sanitary fittings
  • Airing and tidying on board

Other services

  • Tender washing
  • Light maintenance
  • Sail storage
  • Post-boatyard clean-up

We don't clean the keel underwater. Everything else can be done afloat or ashore.

Products

We use eco-friendly products only. You can choose between two lines.

Classic line

Eco-friendly products for routine cleaning, at a lower cost.

Recommended

Nanotech · recommended

It costs more and gives a better result: the surface stays protected for longer and gets dirty more slowly. This is the line we recommend.

Travel to your berth is charged separately and always stated in the quote. For ports outside this list, just ask.

How to get a quote

We work on request, with no booking deadlines. Timing depends on the boat and its condition.

  1. Fill in the form

    Model, length, port and the services you need.

  2. Send photos

    By WhatsApp, so we can see the condition of the boat straight away.

  3. Get the quote

    Covering the services you chose, the product line and travel.

  4. Book the date

    We agree a day and come aboard.
