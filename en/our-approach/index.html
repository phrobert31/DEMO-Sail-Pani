Our story

Sail&Clean is a small craft business offering careful, thorough services for looking after boats.

The idea was born during Covid and has its roots at Asinara. The first contact with boating came during a period at sea, supporting researchers studying whales and dolphins and marine habitats. That time shaped a conscious attitude towards the sea and the environment it protects: a more sustainable outlook that reaches into many areas.

The experience continued aboard a charter boat, working as crew, with a first taste of hard work and life on board. Over the years the work grew into the role of skipper: learning to live alongside very different people and, above all, building a strong relationship with clients.

Then came the first professional deliveries, on routes around Sardinia and between Sardinia and mainland Italy, and with them the first real responsibilities.

Through the Lega Navale (the Italian naval league) we met people who live by the sea, people who shaped our maritime culture and enriched us as people and as seafarers. Finally, we gained experience in boatyards, working alongside riggers on sailing boats.

Values

Sail&Clean is a young, dynamic business, born from bringing together handcraft and boating.

Working with our hands, the gesture itself, is in our view the essence of the job. Handwork, touching things directly, forces you to understand what you are doing and what you are holding: the feel of smooth, clean teak, of an even coat of varnish, of well-treated steel, of a clean line that runs freely, and the sound of a well-oiled mechanism.

The gesture, in repeating an action until it becomes experience: brushing along the grain of the wood, polishing carefully so the result lasts, taking a mechanism apart, putting its parts in order and looking after it.

Being craftsmen, working with small numbers, does not mean giving up on growth. It means being able to nurture precision and dedication.

Products

Our choice of products comes from experience in using them, and depends on the specific surfaces involved and on the condition of the boat.

Cleaning is not only removing a stain, adding shine or simply enjoying the feeling of clean. Cleaning means looking after what we have, for ourselves and our wellbeing, and so for our boat too.

Why not the old home remedy

Why choose a product instead of the old home remedy? The right product is a balance between what we want and what the material needs, depending on how worn or degraded it is. New gel coat cannot be treated the same way as older, worn gel coat, and we cannot expect the same results. Old gel coat, for example, cannot take the same aggressive products as new gel coat: instead of cleaning and removing the dirt, we would only end up damaging the gel coat itself.

Many people clean surfaces with bleach or degreasers. Do they work? They certainly satisfy our sense of clean, because we see a spotless surface. But are they really useful? Along with the surface dirt, these products also corrode the surface itself and, over time, leave it more and more worn and brittle.

An example: mould on tender tubes

A striking example is using bleach to remove mould. Take mouldy tender tubes: many people mix up a bleach solution or use it neat. It removes the mould, but how? On PVC or neoprene, bleach corrodes the whole top layer, lifts the mould and drags it across the entire tender. The result is a tube that is more corroded, less elastic and ages early, with the mould set to come back the following year.

A specific product for mould, on the other hand, protects the material and removes the mould completely: it contains a substance that traps the mould, which is then carried off the boat simply with water.

Mould on tenders is just one of many examples. Besides the hands-on cleaning work, Sail&Clean wants to spread a more conscious use of the right products for boats, both to protect them and out of greater care for the environment around us.

Technology and nanotechnology

For all these reasons we follow new technologies, new methods and developments in our field closely, because the goal is always to offer the best available on the market.

We pay particular attention to nanotechnology, for the value of the products themselves and for how well they adapt to each client's needs. If your boat is a work tool and you need cleaning that is quicker but just as effective, products that create a protective film on the surface save time on daily cleaning and leave more room to rest after work. If you are after top performance because you love speed, we can offer hull sanding with high-end products, also used in international racing and at Olympic level, for which the manufacturer states a 45% gain in efficiency.

These are just some of the examples Sail&Clean offers its clients, aiming at their wellbeing through better care for their boat.
