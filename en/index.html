Why Sail&Clean

Done by hand

A few jobs at a time, so every detail gets attention: teak brushed along the grain, well-treated steel, a clean line that runs freely.

The right product, not the harshest

We choose products for each surface and its condition. No bleach or harsh degreasers that damage gel coat, teak and rubber.

There when you need us

You talk to the people who come aboard: we answer the phone and we work on your boat, always keeping up with new products and methods.
